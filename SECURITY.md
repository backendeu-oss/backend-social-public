# Security Policy

## Scope

This policy covers the official Backend Social web service and Android application currently distributed through the official website and Google Play.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected security vulnerability. Use the official contact route provided in the application or on the Backend Social website, and include only the information needed to reproduce the issue safely.

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, authentication tokens, private messages, or other sensitive account data.

## What to include

- A concise description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce it
- The affected platform and app version, when known
- The expected and actual behavior
- A proof of concept that does not access other users' data

## Disclosure

Please give the maintainer reasonable time to investigate and remediate a valid report before publicly disclosing it. Backend Social reviews reports in good faith and prioritizes issues with a credible impact on account security, privacy, or service availability.

## Out of scope

Social engineering, service disruption, automated mass scanning, or attempts to access accounts or data that do not belong to you are not authorized.
