# Roadmap

This roadmap reflects current product directions. It is not a promise of delivery dates or specific features.

## Current focus

- Improve the reliability of real-time conversations across Android and web sessions
- Make first-time onboarding clearer and easier to revisit
- Continue polishing accessibility, responsive layouts, and language coverage
- Improve feedback collection without asking users to expose private conversation content
- Monitor stability, performance, and account-security signals

## Next areas to explore

- More discovery and sharing options that preserve user control
- Better notification preferences and delivery transparency
- Additional quality-of-life tools for conversations and account management
- Progressive releases and staged feature availability when appropriate

## Principles

Backend Social aims to remain simple, privacy-conscious, and understandable. Feature work is evaluated against those principles before release.
