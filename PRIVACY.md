# Privacy Overview

This page is a plain-language overview for the public repository. It does not replace the current privacy policy shown in Backend Social.

## Principle

Backend Social is built around practical privacy controls and data minimization. Product decisions are intended to give users understandable choices over how they are found, contacted, and notified.

## User controls

Depending on the current release, users can manage preferences such as:

- Who can contact them through private messages
- Whether they appear in search
- Whether their online status is visible
- Which devices and sessions may access their account
- Notification and local application preferences

## What is never published here

This public documentation repository does not contain user messages, account credentials, recovery material, authentication secrets, internal infrastructure configuration, or production database access.

## Official policy

The in-app and official website privacy policy are the authoritative sources. Please review them before using the service, as the policy may change when product functionality or legal requirements change.
